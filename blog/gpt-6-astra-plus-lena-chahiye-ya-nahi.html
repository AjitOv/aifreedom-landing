GPT-6 Astra Aa Gaya — Plus Lena Chahiye Ya Nahi? (2026)

👨‍💻 Ajit (AI Finance Guy) 📅 Sep 6, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Shukrawar ko meri Nashik batch mein pehla sawaal yahi tha: "Sir, GPT-6 aa gaya, ab Plus lena padega kya?" Maine kaha — pehle OpenAI ka help page padho, phir wallet nikaalo. Kyunki jo headline mein likha hai aur jo Plus user ko actually milega, dono alag hain.

Yeh post usi 20-minute discussion ka likha hua version hai. Koi hype nahi — sirf OpenAI ke apne pages se verify kiye hue facts, aur ek seedha jawab.

GPT-6 Astra kya hai (bina benchmark bhashan ke) · Kis plan mein actually milta hai · Free plan ab kya deta hai · 4 self-test prompts jo batayenge upgrade chahiye ya nahi · Ek Nashik MSME example · Mera seedha opinion

Pehle fact, phir opinion: kis plan mein kya mil raha hai

OpenAI ne 3 September 2026 ko GPT-6 Astra announce kiya. Announcement page kehta hai ki "over the coming days" yeh Plus, Pro, Business aur Enterprise users ko milega. Yahi line har news site ne copy ki.

Lekin OpenAI ke help center page — jo aaj subah update hua — mein detail alag hai:

  • Free / Go: Chat mein GPT-6 nahi. Model hai GPT-5.6 Luna.
  • Plus ($20/month): Chat mein GPT-6 nahi — GPT-5.6 Sol hi rahega. Astra sirf "Work" aur Codex mein, woh bhi "limited Astra usage".
  • Pro $100: Haan — "GPT-6 Pro", 50 messages per week.
  • Pro $200: Haan — 200 messages per week.
  • Business Standard: 15 messages per month, shared allowance.

Do cheezein dhyan se padho. Ek: Plus user ko normal Chat window mein GPT-6 nahi milega. Sirf Work mode (jo agent hai — documents, spreadsheets, browsing ke liye) aur Codex (coding) mein, woh bhi limited. Do: Pro $200 wale ko bhi hafte mein 200 messages ki limit hai. "Unlimited GPT-6" naam ki cheez kisi plan mein nahi hai.

Prices OpenAI ke apne page se: Go $8, Plus $20, Pro $200 per month, aur ek Pro $100 tier bhi hai. India mein rupee pricing Indian card/UPI se alag dikhti hai aur baar-baar badalti hai — main yahan koi ₹ figure nahi likh raha jo kal galat ho jaaye. Upgrade se pehle chatgpt.com/pricing khud kholo, Indian payment method ke saath.

GPT-6 Astra actually kya karta hai (jo aapke kaam ka hai)

Benchmark numbers skip karta hoon — 98% FrontierMath aapke kirana ke stock sheet mein kaam nahi aayega. Jo cheezein OpenAI ne apne page pe dikhayi aur jo real users ke kaam ki hain:

Computer use. Astra khud browser aur apps chala sakta hai — online form bharna, CRM mein customer record update karna, calendar set karna. OpenAI ka apna number: OSWorld test mein 72.6% score, average 40 minute per task, jabki pichla model 65.7% aur 75 minute leta tha. Matlab agent kaam pehle se tez aur zyada sahi. Lekin yeh Work mode ki baat hai, Chat ki nahi.

Template follow karna. Astra "follows your templates and instructions" — aapki company ka PPT template do, usi format mein slides banata hai. Corporate batches mein yeh sabse zyada maanga jaane wala feature hai, aur pehle ke models ismein kamzor the.

Kam galat claims. OpenAI ka internal hallucination benchmark: Astra 4.2%, pichla model 12.2%. Better hai. Zero nahi hai. 100 mein 4 baar abhi bhi galat bolega — GST rate, exam date, dawai ka dose — cross-check karna band mat karo.

Ek naya failure mode. OpenAI ke release notes kehte hain Astra mein "additional safety monitoring" hai — agar system ko lage ki agent ne instruction galat samjha, toh "the conversation may be paused or stopped." Matlab aapka 30-minute wala Work task beech mein ruk sakta hai, aur aapko manually review karke aage badhana hoga. Deadline pe kaam de rahe ho toh plan karke rakho.

Aur ek line help page se: "Astra can use your allowance faster than GPT-5.6 Sol." Plus ka limited Work allowance Astra ke saath jaldi khatam hoga. Maine ek student ko dekha jo purane model pe hafte bhar Work chalata tha — naye model pe wahi allowance 2 din mein khatam.

Free plan ab kya deta hai — yeh part log skip karte hain

Yahi post ka sabse important section hai. OpenAI ke help page ke mutabik Free aur Go users ko:

  • Unlimited everyday text chats (abuse guardrails ke saath). Pehle free users ko har 3-4 ghante mein limit lagti thi. Ab text ke liye nahi.
  • Think button — mushkil sawaal ke liye reasoning mode. Model GPT-5.6 Luna hai, Sol nahi, lekin school/college level reasoning ke liye kaafi hai.
  • Limits abhi bhi lagti hain file upload, image generation, voice, aur data analysis pe.

Mere 15 saal ke training experience mein 80% log ChatGPT se sirf yeh karte hain: email likhwana, notes banwana, resume sudhaarna, WhatsApp replies, kisi topic ko samajhna, translate karna. Yeh sab text chat hai. Yeh sab ab free mein unlimited hai.

4 prompts jo batayenge — aapko upgrade chahiye ya nahi

Yeh prompts free ChatGPT mein chalao (Think on karke). Agar chaaron ka output aapke kaam ke liye theek hai, toh aapko is mahine Plus nahi chahiye. Agar 2 ya zyada fail hote hain, tab neeche wala section padho.

Test 1 — Aapka real daily kaam

Main [aapka kaam — e.g. Nashik mein tiles ki shop chalata hoon]. Mera roz ka sabse repetitive likhne ka kaam hai [e.g. customers ko WhatsApp pe quotation bhejna]. Yeh raha ek real example: [paste karo]. Isko professional banao, Hinglish mein rakho, 60 words se kam, aur end mein ek line add karo jo customer ko aaj decide karne ki wajah de. 3 variations do.

Test 2 — Ek document jo aap actually use karoge

Mujhe [B.Com 2nd year] ke liye [Cost Accounting, Unit 3: Marginal Costing] ke revision notes chahiye. Format: har concept ke liye — 1-line definition, 1 formula, 1 solved example Indian rupees mein, aur 1 common exam mistake. Hindi terms bracket mein do jahan zaroori ho. Sirf woh cheezein jo university exam mein poochi jaati hain.

Test 3 — Reasoning (Think button on karo)

Mere paas ₹40,000 ka monthly budget hai ek 4-person marketing team ke liye. Options: (a) ek freelancer ₹25,000 + tools ₹15,000, (b) do part-time interns ₹12,000 each + tools ₹16,000. Team ko har mahine 20 social posts, 4 blogs, aur 2 email campaigns chahiye. Dono options ke liye output quality, risk, aur 6-month cost compare karo. Pehle assumptions list karo, phir recommendation do, phir batao kis situation mein aap galat ho sakte ho.

Test 4 — Woh kaam jo aapko lagta hai AI nahi kar sakta

Yeh mera [last month ka sales data / class ka result sheet / vendor list] hai: [paste karo, 20-30 rows]. Isme 3 patterns dhoondo jo mujhe khud nahi dikhe. Har pattern ke liye batao: kya hai, kitna sure ho (percent mein), aur main ise verify kaise karoon.

Test 4 aksar free plan pe fail hota hai — data analysis pe limit hai aur Luna long tables pe kamzor hai. Agar aapka roz ka kaam yahi hai, tab paid plan sochne layak hai. Baaki teen tests pe free plan mere batches mein pass hota hai.

Worked example: Nashik ka tiles dealer, ₹0 ka plan

Mere ek MSME batch mein Rajesh (naam badla hai) hain — Nashik Road pe tiles aur sanitary ka showroom. Roz 15-20 quotation WhatsApp pe. Unhone Test 1 free ChatGPT mein chalaya. Input:

Main Nashik mein tiles ki shop chalata hoon. Mera roz ka repetitive kaam hai customers ko WhatsApp pe quotation bhejna. Real example: "Sir 2x2 vitrified 600 sq ft ka rate 42 per sqft hai, total 25200, delivery alag, GST extra." Isko professional banao, Hinglish mein, 60 words se kam, end mein ek line jo aaj decide karne ki wajah de. 3 variations do.

Output ka pehla variation (jo unhone actually use kiya):

Namaste Sir 🙏 Aapke 600 sq ft ke liye 2x2 Vitrified tiles ka quotation: ₹42/sq ft × 600 = ₹25,200 (+18% GST, delivery alag). Same lot se colour matching guaranteed hai — aaj confirm karenge toh yeh lot aapke liye hold kar dunga, kal stock rotate ho jaata hai. — Rajesh, [Shop Name]

Do cheezein note karo. AI ne "18% GST" khud daala — Rajesh ne check kiya, tiles pe 18% sahi hai, lekin yeh check karna unka kaam tha, AI ka nahi. Aur "same lot se colour matching" wali line AI ne banayi — yeh sach mein tiles business ka real concern hai, isliye chali. Rajesh ne is template ko ek baar bana ke ChatGPT ki memory mein save karwa diya; ab sirf numbers badalte hain. Cost: ₹0. Plus ki zaroorat: nahi.

Kaun upgrade kare — sirf yeh 3 log

Plus ($20/month) lo agar: aap roz files upload karte ho (PDF, Excel), ya Work mode mein documents/spreadsheets banwane hain, ya coding karte ho (Codex). Sirf "GPT-6 ke liye" Plus mat lo — Chat mein aapko woh milega hi nahi.

Pro ($100 ya $200) lo agar: aap professional ho jiska kaam hi AI agent chalana hai — research, multi-step automation, CAD/data science. Aur $200 wale ko bhi 200 messages/week hai. Freelancer jo mahine ke ₹50,000+ AI-assisted kaam se kamata hai, uske liye math ban sakta hai. Student ke liye nahi.

Developer ho toh: API pe Astra $10 per million input tokens, $50 per million output tokens. Pehle Luna/Terra pe test karo, Astra sirf wahan jahan quality difference dikhe.

Baaki sab — student, teacher, shopkeeper, HR exec jo CV screen karta hai, accountant jo GST reminders bhejta hai — free plan pe raho. Unlimited text chat aur Think button aapke kaam ke liye kaafi hai. December tak wait karo; tab tak rollout complete hoga, limits clear hongi, aur India ki rupee pricing settle hogi. Tab decide karna.

Mera opinion, jo aapko doosri jagah nahi milega

Har AI blog aaj "GPT-6 is here, upgrade now" likh raha hai. Main ulta keh raha hoon: is hafte ek bhi rupaya mat do. Teen wajah.

Ek, Plus user ko headline wala model Chat mein milta hi nahi — yeh OpenAI ke apne help page pe likha hai, kisi ne padha nahi. Do, free plan pichle 2 mahine mein itna improve hua hai (unlimited text, Think button) ki paid ka gap 80% users ke liye band ho gaya. Teen, naya model launch ke pehle 2 hafte hamesha rollout issues, allowance confusion aur safety pauses ke saath aata hai — early adopter banne ka koi inaam nahi hai jab aapka kaam email aur notes hai.

Model ka naam mat kharido. Apna kaam dekho, upar ke 4 test chalao, phir decide karo.
