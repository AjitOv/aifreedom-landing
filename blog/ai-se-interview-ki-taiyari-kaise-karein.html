AI Se Interview Ki Taiyari Kaise Karein — 5 Mock Prompts (2026)

👨‍💻 Ajit (AI Finance Guy) 📅 Sep 4, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Last month ki corporate batch mein 40 log the — sabke resume ChatGPT se bane hue, sab clean, sab ATS-friendly. Phir maine 5 logon ka mock interview liya. Pehla sawaal: "Aapke resume mein likha hai 'data-driven decision making' — ek example do." Paanch mein se teen chup. Resume AI ne likha tha, interview unhe dena tha.

Yahi gap is post ka subject hai. Naukri JobSpeak ke June 2026 report ke mutabik fresher hiring 8% badhi hai year-on-year — matlab resume shortlist ho rahe hain, interviews zyada ho rahe hain. Bottleneck ab interview hai. Aur interview mein AI ka sahi use "answers likhwana" nahi hai. Sahi use hai: AI ko interviewer banana.

💡 Is post mein milega:
AI se answers kyun nahi likhwane · 5 copy-paste mock interview prompts · ChatGPT Voice aur Gemini Live se bolne ki practice (free mein) · ek B.Com fresher ka pura example · 4 jagah jahan AI galat hota hai · freshers ki 5 galtiyan

Pehli baat: AI se answer likhwana sabse mehenga shortcut hai

Har dusra blog kehta hai "ChatGPT se 'Tell me about yourself' ka answer likhwao aur yaad kar lo." Main iske against hoon, aur is baat par mujhse log disagree karte hain.

Wajah simple hai. Jo HR ek din mein 40 candidates dekhta hai, usne "I am a quick learner and a team player who is passionate about..." subah se 30 baar suna hai. AI ka default answer sabka same hota hai, kyunki sabne same prompt diya hai. AI-written answer bolte hi aap crowd mein ghul jaate ho.

Doosri wajah: yaad kiya hua answer ek follow-up par toot jaata hai. "Achha, aap quick learner ho — last 6 mahine mein kya seekha?" Yahan script khatam, aur candidate ka chehra bhi.

Isliye pura process ulta karo. AI se answer mat maango. AI se sawaal maango, dabav maango, feedback maango. Answer aapka hona chahiye — unpolished bhi chalega, lekin aapka.

Step-by-step: AI ko apna interviewer banao

Step 1 — Context do (JD + resume, dono)

Bina context ke AI generic sawaal puchhega — "What are your strengths?" type. Job description aur apna resume dono paste karo. Yeh ek baar karna hai; baaki saare prompts isi chat mein chalenge. (Resume abhi tak nahi bana? Pehle yeh post padho.)

Main [role, e.g., Accounts Executive] ke interview ki taiyari kar raha/rahi hoon, company: [company name], location: [city]. Neeche job description aur mera resume hai. Abhi kuch mat likho — sirf dono padh kar 3 lines mein batao: is JD ke hisaab se mere resume ka sabse strong point kya hai aur sabse weak point kya hai.

JOB DESCRIPTION: [paste karo]

MERA RESUME: [paste karo]

Weak point wala hissa zaroori hai. Interviewer wahi poochega.

Step 2 — Question bank banwao, answers nahi

Is JD aur resume ke basis par 15 interview questions banao, 3 groups mein: (a) 5 questions jo mere resume ke specific points par hain, (b) 5 technical/role-based questions jo [role] ke liye is level par poochhe jaate hain, (c) 5 HR questions. Har question ke saamne ek line likho: interviewer yeh kyun pooch raha hai. Answers mat likhna.

"Answers mat likhna" bina likhe AI phir bhi answers likh deta hai. Likho.

Ab in 15 questions ke answers khud likho — kaagaz par, 2–3 line har ek ka. Bura likho, koi baat nahi. Yeh aapka raw material hai.

Step 3 — Mock interview mode (ek sawaal, ek jawab)

Yeh main prompt hai. Copy karo:

Ab tum [company name] ke interviewer ho, [role] ke liye. Rules: (1) Ek baar mein sirf ek sawaal poochho. (2) Mere jawab ke baad pehle 2 line mein honest feedback do — kya kaam kiya, kya nahi. (3) Agar mera jawab vague hai toh follow-up poochho, aage mat badho. (4) Polite mat raho; ek busy Indian manager ki tarah behave karo jiske paas 20 minute hain. (5) 8 sawaalon ke baad 10 mein se score do aur 3 sabse zaroori sudhaar batao. Shuru karo.

Rule 4 sabse important hai. Bina iske ChatGPT aapke har jawab ko "Great answer" kahega, jo practice ke liye bekaar hai.

Step 4 — Bol kar practice karo (typing se interview nahi hota)

Type karke aap sochne ka time le lete ho. Interview mein woh time nahi milta. Isliye Step 3 wala prompt voice mein chalao.

Do free options, dono maine aaj OpenAI aur Google ke apne help pages par check kiye:

  • ChatGPT Voice (app ya web par mic icon): OpenAI ke help page ke mutabik Free plan par "Live" voice GPT-Live-1 mini par chalta hai, har rolling 24 ghante mein limited access. Go/Plus par 1 ghanta GPT-Live-1 aur 2 ghante mini. Ek Live conversation zyada se zyada 2 ghante ka ho sakta hai. Mock interview ke liye 20–25 minute kaafi hain, toh free plan chal jaata hai.
  • Gemini Live (Gemini mobile app, Android/iPhone — web app par nahi): Google ka help page khud "Practice aloud: Rehearse for important moments" ko use case batata hai. Requirement sirf app aur ek personal Google account — paid plan ka koi zikr nahi. Beech mein interrupt kar sakte ho, aur End karne par pura transcript milta hai — woh transcript Step 5 mein kaam aata hai.

Tip: voice mein Step 3 ka lamba prompt bolna mushkil hai. Pehle text mein prompt paste karo, phir usi chat mein voice on karo. (Kaunsa tool kab — ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude wali post mein detail hai.)

Step 5 — Transcript se scorecard

Mock khatam hone ke baad transcript (ya apne likhe answers) wapas do:

Yeh mere mock interview ka transcript hai. Ismein se batao: (1) Kaunse 3 phrases maine baar-baar repeat kiye? (2) Kaunse jawab 30 second se lambe the aur unhe kahan kaatna chahiye? (3) Kis jawab mein maine claim kiya lekin example nahi diya? (4) Ek jawab choose karo jo sabse kamzor tha aur mujhe 3 pointed questions do jinke jawab dene se woh answer khud ban jayega. Mere liye answer mat likhna.

TRANSCRIPT: [paste karo]

Point 4 dhyan se padho. AI answer nahi de raha — aapse woh sawaal pooch raha hai jinke jawab se aapka answer banega. Yahi farak hai ratne aur taiyari mein.

Ek pura example: Sneha, B.Com final year, Nashik

Chalo ek scenario end-to-end chalate hain — situation meri weekend batches se bahut familiar hai.

Sneha ka interview ek CA firm mein Accounts Executive ke liye hai. JD mein Tally, GST returns, bank reconciliation. Resume mein "GST filing ka knowledge" likha hai, kyunki college project mein ek baar dekha tha.

Step 1 mein AI weak point pakadta hai: "Resume says GST filing knowledge, but no evidence of actual filing. Expect a question here."

Step 3 mock mein teesra sawaal: "Aapne GSTR-1 aur GSTR-3B mein difference bataya — ab batao, agar client ka ek invoice GSTR-1 mein miss ho gaya toh next month kya karoge?"

Sneha ka jawab: "Sir main senior se poochh lungi."

AI ka feedback (Rule 2 ki wajah se): "Honest, but the interviewer wanted to see whether you know a missed invoice can be added in a later return. Say what you know first, then say you'd confirm the exact process with a senior. Right now you've shown zero knowledge and full dependence."

Step 5 mein AI usse 3 sawaal deta hai — "Missed invoice ka client par kya asar hota hai? Tumne college project mein kaunsa return dekha tha? Usmein amendment ka option kahan tha?" — aur Sneha in teen ke jawab jod kar apna answer khud banati hai. Ratta nahi, samajh.

Real interview mein wahi sawaal thoda alag form mein aayega. Goal yeh hai ki interview mein ek bhi sawaal "naya" na lage.

4 jagah jahan AI aapko dhokha dega

1. Company ke facts. "Tell me about our company" ki taiyari AI se mat karo. Poochhoge toh AI confidently revenue, founding year, clients bata dega — aadhe galat. Maine students ko AI ka banaya hua "Pune office 2019 mein khula" interview mein bolte dekha hai. Company ki website, LinkedIn page aur Google News — sirf yahi source hain. AI se sirf yeh poochho ki website padh kar kya notes banane chahiye.

2. Bhasha. AI ke answers American corporate English mein aate hain — "I leveraged cross-functional synergies." Nashik ki CA firm mein yeh bologe toh interviewer muskurayega, achhe wale tareeke se nahi. Prompt mein add karo: "Simple Indian English, jaise ek fresher bolta hai."

3. Zyada polite interviewer. Rule 4 ke bina AI har jawab ko sarahega. Agar mock mein 8 mein se 8 answers "excellent" aaye, toh prompt galat hai, aap nahi.

4. Voice mein Hinglish. ChatGPT Voice aur Gemini Live dono Hinglish ko kabhi-kabhi galat sunte hain, khaaskar technical words ("GSTR-3B" kuch bhi ban sakta hai). Captions on rakho aur transcript check karo. OpenAI ka page khud kehta hai ki voice transcript verbatim nahi hota.

Freshers ke liye: 5 galtiyan jo main har batch mein dekhta hoon

  1. Poore 15 questions ke AI answers yaad karna. Upar padh liya kyun nahi. Ek follow-up par sab girta hai.
  2. Sirf HR questions ki practice. Fresher ko lagta hai technical round mein "basic" poochhenge. Step 2 ke group (b) questions skip mat karo — CA firm Tally poochegi, IT company loop aur SQL join poochegi.
  3. Ek hi mock. Pehla mock hamesha bura hota hai; woh baseline hai. Teen mock, teen alag din — minimum. Score 10 mein se 4 se 7 tak jaata hai, yeh pattern maine baar-baar dekha hai.
  4. Mock sirf text mein. Step 4 skip karna matlab aadhi taiyari. Bolne mein "umm", speed aur confidence ka pata chalta hai, typing mein nahi.
  5. Paid "AI interview coach" apps. Unka subscription jo bhi ho, upar ke 5 prompts free ChatGPT aur Gemini app par wahi kaam karte hain. Pehle yeh karo, phir sochna paise kahan lagane hain.

Aaj raat ka homework: Step 1 aur Step 2 — 20 minute. Kal Step 3 ka pehla mock, score note karo. Teesre mock ke baad batao kya badla.
