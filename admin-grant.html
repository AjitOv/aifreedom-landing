Grant course access

For customers who paid outside Razorpay — UPI, WhatsApp, bank transfer or cash. Anyone who paid through the site should use “Restore my access” with their Payment ID instead.

Set in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables. Never stored in this page.
Stored inside the token so a manual grant is always distinguishable from a purchase.
Treat the link like a password. Anyone who opens it gets the course. Send it to one person, privately. There is no database here, so keep your own record of who you granted and why — the Note field is written into the token to help.
